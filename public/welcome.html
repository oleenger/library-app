Questions

The things readers ask us.

How does the photo intake work?+

You photograph a bookshelf and a vision model reads the spines, transcribing exactly what's printed — even unfamiliar or non-English titles — then best-guesses the period and movement from a controlled taxonomy. Every row is presented for your review before anything is saved, so a confident wrong guess never sneaks in.

What's the difference between a work and an edition?+

A work is the thing you read — say, Crime and Punishment. An edition is a particular printing you own. The Library keeps them separate, so an omnibus that holds several works, or a novel you own in two translations, is understood correctly instead of collapsed into a duplicate.

Can I bring my existing catalogue over?+

Yes. Import a CSV of your collection or a Goodreads export. Reading history is matched to the right work automatically — including across translations — and everything is validated against the taxonomy on the way in.

Where do the recommendations come from?+

Two places: your taste (what to read next, drawn from what you own and have read) and canonical gaps (major works your library is missing). They're generated only when your library changes, so they stay fresh without ever feeling random.

Is my data private?+

Your library is single-owner by design and gated to your account. We don't sell data, we don't run ads, and you can export everything you've built at any time.
